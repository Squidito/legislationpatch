Signed Into Law — 119th Congress

Six of the 119th Congress bills tracked in the LegislationPatch database are signed into law:

S. 4465 — FISA Further Extension to June 12, 2026
Signed April 30, 2026. Extends Section 702 surveillance authority through June 12, 2026. No substantive changes to the underlying authority.

H.J.Res. 140 — CRA/BLM Land Withdrawal Disapproval
Signed April 27, 2026. Cancels the BLM's 20-year mining ban on 225,504 acres of Minnesota National Forest land bordering the Boundary Waters.

H.R. 8322 — FISA Extension to April 30, 2026
Signed April 18, 2026. Short-term extension of FISA Section 702 authority by 12 days.

S. 1884 — Holocaust Expropriated Art Recovery Act of 2025
Signed April 13, 2026 (Pub. L. 119-82). Permanently removes procedural barriers to Holocaust art restitution claims in U.S. courts.

S. 3971 — Small Business Innovation and Economic Security Act
Signed April 13, 2026. Extends SBIR/STTR grant programs through FY2031 and adds mandatory foreign adversary screening.

What to Watch

FISA Section 702 — lapsed June 12, 2026. The last extension (S. 4465, Pub. L. 119-87) carried Section 702 only through June 12, 2026. After the House rejected a further short-term extension (H.R. 9238) on June 11, no reauthorization was in place and the authority lapsed on June 12 — the first Section 702 lapse since 2008. Collection under certifications approved before the lapse continues until they expire; a longer-term reauthorization remains unresolved.

FY2027 appropriations — deadline September 30, 2026. Current government funding expires when the fiscal year ends. Based on historical patterns, a continuing resolution or omnibus will be required. The appropriations process typically intensifies in July and August.
