What does the Faster Labor Contracts Act do?

HR 5408 is a House bill sponsored by Rep. Donald Norcross (D-NJ). Speeds up first union contracts: it sets deadlines to start bargaining, then routes unresolved disputes to mediation and, if needed, binding arbitration that sets the contract for two years. Sets deadlines and a mediation-then-binding-arbitration process for reaching a first union contract under the National Labor Relations Act.

Did HR 5408 pass? Where it stands

As of August 13, 2026, HR 5408 has passed the House.

Status: Passed House

Latest vote: House Passed 230–193 on June 9, 2026

Outlook: Possible

Key provisions

  • First Contract Deadlines
    • Bargaining must begin within 10 days of a union's request
    • Mediation available if no deal after 90 days
  • Binding Arbitration
    • Unresolved disputes go to a 3-person arbitration panel
    • Panel decision binds both sides for 2 years
