HR 1687 · Passed House · 06-03-26

CLEAN Act

Rep. Fulcher, Russ (R-ID) · 4 cosponsors · 1 page

Status: Passed House

Latest vote: House Passed (06-02-26)

Outlook: Possible

This bill amends the Geothermal Steam Act of 1970 to make the federal government hold geothermal energy lease sales more often and on a tighter schedule. It requires lease sales every year instead of every two years, requires a replacement sale in the same year if a scheduled sale is canceled or delayed, and sets 30-day deadlines for the Interior Department to act on geothermal drilling permit applications.

Requires annual federal geothermal lease sales (up from every two years), mandates replacement sales for canceled ones, and imposes 30-day permit-review deadlines.

Key provisions

  • More Frequent Lease Sales
    • Geothermal lease sales required every year instead of every 2 years
    • Canceled or delayed sales must be replaced with another sale the same year
    • Interior must offer all nominated parcels eligible under the state resource management plan
  • Permit Decision Deadlines
    • Interior must confirm an application is complete (or list missing items) within 30 days
    • Final decision on a complete drilling-permit application due within 30 days of completeness notice
