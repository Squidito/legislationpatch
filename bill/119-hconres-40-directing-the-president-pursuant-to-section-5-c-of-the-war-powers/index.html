HCONRES 40 · Failed in House · 04-16-26

Directing the President, pursuant to section 5(c) of the War Powers Resolution, to remove United States Armed Forces from hostilities with Iran.

Rep. Meeks, Gregory W. (D-NY) · 90 cosponsors · 1 page

What does the HCONRES 40 do?

HCONRES 40 is a House concurrent resolution sponsored by Rep. Gregory W. Meeks (D-NY). Directs the President to remove United States Armed Forces from hostilities against the Islamic Republic of Iran under section 5(c) of the War Powers Resolution (50 U.S.C. 1544(c)). Forces required to defend the United States, an ally, or a partner from imminent attack may remain, provided the President fully complies with the 60-day withdrawal requirement in section 5(b) of the War Powers Resolution. Continued operations against Iran require either a declaration of war or a specific congressional authorization for use of military force.
