HR 8874 · Passed House · 07-13-26

Homeland Security Capabilities Preservation Reporting Act of 2026

Rep. Carter, Troy A. (D-LA-2) · 2 cosponsors · 1 page

What does the HR 8874 do?

HR 8874 is a House bill sponsored by Rep. Troy A. Carter (D-LA-2). Amends paragraph (1) of section 7102(b) of the James M. Inhofe National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2023 (Public Law 117-263) so that the report on jurisdictions no longer eligible for Urban Area Security Initiative funding must be produced "every three years thereafter," not just once within 18 months of that Act's enactment. Turns a one-time report on jurisdictions losing Urban Area Security Initiative eligibility into a recurring one issued every three years.

Did HR 8874 pass? Where it stands
