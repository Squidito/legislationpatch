HR 5578 · Passed House · 07-21-26

Expanding Whistleblower Protections for Contractors Act of 2025

Rep. Garcia, Robert (D-CA-42) · 1 cosponsor · 6 pages

What does the HR 5578 do?

HR 5578 is a House bill sponsored by Rep. Robert Garcia (D-CA-42). 10 U.S.C. 4701 protects Department of Defense and NASA contractor employees from being fired, demoted, or discriminated against for disclosing evidence of wrongdoing in defense contracts; 41 U.S.C. 4712 does the same for non-defense federal contractors. This bill rewrites both statutes to swap "employee" for a new defined term "protected individual" — which explicitly covers state, tribal, territorial, and personal-services contractors and intelligence-community elements within DoD — expands the covered protected activities to include refusing to obey unlawful orders and disclosing gross mismanagement or gross waste, prohibits executive branch officials from requesting a reprisal, authorizes disciplinary action against officials who do, and bars waiver of the rights, forum, and remedies by any agreement including predispute arbitration.

Key provisions

  • New "Protected Individual" Class
    • Replaces "employee" throughout 10 U.S.C. 4701 and 41 U.S.C. 4712 with "protected individual"
    • Covers contractors, subcontractors, grantees, and subgrantees of DoD, NASA, and (in §4712) the Federal Government
    • Covers state, DC, tribal, Puerto Rico, Guam, American Samoa, Virgin Islands, N. Mariana Islands, and other territorial governments
    • Covers political subdivisions, agencies, and instrumentalities of those governments
    • Covers intelligence community elements within DoD (§4701) and generally (§4712) — defined by 50 U.S.C. 3003
    • Covers employees, former employees, and personal-services contractors of the above
  • Expanded Protected Activities
    • Refusing to obey an order that would require violating a law, rule, or regulation
    • Disclosing gross mismanagement of a DoD or NASA (or Federal) contract or grant
    • Disclosing gross waste of Department funds or a substantial and specific danger to public health or safety
    • Disclosing abuse of authority or violation of law related to any contract, subcontract, grant, or subgrant
  • Executive Branch Reprisal-Request Ban
    • Executive branch officials may not request that a contractor, subcontractor, grantee, or subgrantee engage in a prohibited reprisal
    • Agencies may propose appropriate disciplinary action against any executive branch official who does
    • Applies to both DoD/NASA (§4701) and non-defense (§4712) programs
  • Non-Waivable Rights and Anti-Arbitration
    • Rights, forum, and remedies may not be waived by any public or private agreement
    • Includes any policy, form, or condition of employment
    • Explicitly bars predispute arbitration agreements as a waiver mechanism
