Protect America's Workforce Act

Rep. Golden, Jared F. (D-ME-2) · 226 cosponsors · 1 page

What does the Protect America's Workforce Act do?

HR 2550 is a House bill sponsored by Rep. Jared F. Golden (D-ME-2). Section 2 declares the March 27, 2025 Executive Order titled "Exclusions from Federal Labor-Management Relations Programs" of no force or effect and bars any federal funds from being obligated or expended to carry it out. Section 3 preserves any collective bargaining agreement in effect as of March 26, 2025 between an executive branch federal agency and an exclusive representative of federal employees through the stated term of that agreement.
