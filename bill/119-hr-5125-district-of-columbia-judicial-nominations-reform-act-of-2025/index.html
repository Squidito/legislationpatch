District of Columbia Judicial Nominations Reform Act of 2025

Rep. Sessions, Pete (R-TX-17) · 2 cosponsors · 1 page

What does the District of Columbia Judicial Nominations Reform Act of 2025 do?

HR 5125 is a House bill sponsored by Rep. Pete Sessions (R-TX-17). Terminates the District of Columbia Judicial Nomination Commission by striking section 434 of the District of Columbia Home Rule Act (sec. 1-204.34, D.C. Official Code). Conforming amendments to section 431(b) hand designation of chief judges to the President, section 433(a) removes the President's obligation to nominate from a Commission list, section 433(b) drops Commission-service credit from the judicial qualifications, and section 11-1528(a)(2) of the D.C. Official Code strikes subparagraph (C). The amendments apply to appointments made on or after the date of enactment.
