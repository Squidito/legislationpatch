FEC Administrative Improvements Act

Rep. Morelle, Joseph D. (D-NY-25) · 3 cosponsors · 1 page

What does the FEC Administrative Improvements Act do?

HR 8738 is a House bill sponsored by Rep. Joseph D. Morelle (D-NY-25). Makes two changes to the Federal Election Campaign Act of 1971. Section 2 amends 52 U.S.C. 30104(a)(11)(A)(i) so that the electronic filing requirement covers persons who "make[] electioneering communications," not just those making expenditures. Section 3 rewrites 52 U.S.C. 30102(h)(1) to let political committees make disbursements by means other than a check drawn on the committee's account. Requires electronic FEC filing for persons making electioneering communications and lets political committees pay by methods other than check.

Key provisions

  • Electronic Filing for Electioneering Communications
    • Amends 52 U.S.C. 30104(a)(11)(A)(i)
    • Inserts "or makes electioneering communications" after "expenditures"
  • Disbursements No Longer Check-Only
    • Amends 52 U.S.C. 30102(h)(1)
    • Strikes "except by check drawn on such accounts in accordance with this section"
    • Inserts "except from such accounts" in its place
