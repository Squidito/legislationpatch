HR 8595 · Passed House · 07-29-26

National Security, Department of State, and Related Programs Appropriations Act, 2027

Rep. Diaz-Balart, Mario (R-FL-26) · 138 pages

What does the HR 8595 do?

HR 8595 is a House bill sponsored by Rep. Mario Diaz-Balart (R-FL-26). This bill provides fiscal year 2027 funding for the Department of State, foreign assistance, and related international programs. It sets specific amounts for diplomacy, global health, humanitarian aid, and security assistance, and attaches policy conditions to many of those funds. Funds the Department of State and U.S. foreign operations for fiscal year 2027.

Did HR 8595 pass? Where it stands

As of August 17, 2026, HR 8595 has passed the House.

Status: Passed House

Outlook: Possible

Key provisions

  • Diplomacy and State Operations
    • $9.76B for Diplomatic Programs
    • Funds embassy operations, worldwide security, and consular activities
  • Global Health and Humanitarian Aid
    • $3.35B for global health and $5.53B for HIV/AIDS programs
    • $1.25B contribution to the Global Fund to Fight AIDS, Tuberculosis and Malaria
    • $5B for international humanitarian and refugee assistance
  • Security and Economic Assistance
    • $6.75B for Foreign Military Financing grants
    • $6.89B for National Security Investment Programs
    • $1.66B for international narcotics control and law enforcement
