ACES Act of 2025

Sen. Kelly, Mark (D-AZ) · 3 cosponsors · 2 pages

What does the ACES Act of 2025 do?

S 201 is a Senate bill sponsored by Sen. Mark Kelly (D-AZ). Orders an independent scientific study of how often cancer occurs, and how often it is fatal, among military members who served as aircrew on fixed-wing aircraft. The Department of Veterans Affairs must arrange for the National Academies of Sciences, Engineering, and Medicine to conduct it.

Did S 201 pass? Where it stands

As of August 7, 2026, S 201 has been signed into law on August 14, 2025.

Key provisions

  • National Academies Cancer Study
    • VA must seek an agreement with the National Academies within 30 days of enactment
    • Study covers cancer prevalence and mortality among fixed-wing aircrew
    • Must report to Congress if the agreement is not finalized within 60 days of negotiations
  • Scope of the Study
    • Identifies exposures tied to aircrew occupations (chemicals, compounds, agents)
    • Reviews 11 named cancer types, including brain, lung, prostate, and thyroid
    • Uses VA, DoD, and CDC death-index data where available
