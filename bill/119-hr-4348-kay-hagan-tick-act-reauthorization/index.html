Kay Hagan Tick Act Reauthorization

What does the Kay Hagan Tick Act Reauthorization do?

HR 4348 is a House bill sponsored by Rep. Christopher H. Smith (R-NJ-4). Section 317U of the Public Health Service Act authorizes a national strategy and regional Centers of Excellence in Vector-Borne Disease at CDC to identify, report, prevent, and respond to vector-borne diseases including tick-borne illnesses; section 2822 authorizes enhanced support to help state and local health departments address vector-borne diseases. Both authorizations lapsed at the end of fiscal year 2025 — this bill reauthorizes them for fiscal years 2026 through 2030, and also replaces the requirement that CDC consult with the statutorily named "Tick-Borne Disease Working Group" with a broader requirement to consult with "appropriate individuals", and adds language requiring the national strategy to address the underlying capacity of state and local health departments to identify, report, prevent, and respond to such diseases.
