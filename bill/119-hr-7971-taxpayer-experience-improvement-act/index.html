HR 7971 · Passed House · 04-28-26

Taxpayer Experience Improvement Act

Rep. Schweikert, David (R-AZ) · 1 cosponsor · 5 pages

Status: Passed House

Latest vote: House Passed (04-27-26)

Outlook: Unlikely

This bill requires the IRS to publish real-time data on its public website about phone wait times, call volumes, and processing backlogs for every public phone line it operates, and to make that data available through a public API so third-party apps can display it. It also requires the IRS to give taxpayers online or mobile access to their own tax returns, notices, and refund status including an estimated delivery date.

Requires the IRS to publish live phone wait times and backlog data, and give taxpayers digital access to their own returns and refund status.

Key provisions

  • Real-Time IRS Dashboard
    • IRS must publish live caller counts, wait times, and callback availability per phone line on its public website
    • Public API required so any person or app can access the live call data
  • Digital Tax Record Access
    • Taxpayers can view all returns, notices, and IRS letters via website or mobile app
    • IRS must provide estimated refund receipt date
  • Monthly Accountability Metrics
    • IRS must publish monthly averages for call length, hold time, disconnection rate, and percent of callers who got help
    • Sense of Congress: callback offered within 5 minutes on any unanswered call by 2028
