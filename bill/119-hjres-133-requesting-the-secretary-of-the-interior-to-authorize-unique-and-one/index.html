HJRES 133 · Signed into Law · 12-02-25

Requesting the Secretary of the Interior to authorize unique and one-time arrangements for displays on the National Mall and the Washington Monument during the period beginning on December 31, 2025, and ending on January 5, 2026

Rep. Aderholt, Robert B. (R-AL) · 3 cosponsors · 2 pages

What does the HJRES 133 do?

HJRES 133 is a House joint resolution sponsored by Rep. Robert B. Aderholt (R-AL). Asks the Interior Department to allow special one-time displays of 250th-anniversary materials on the National Mall — including projections on the Washington Monument for five nights — around New Year's 2025-2026. Requests the Interior Secretary authorize one-time 250th-anniversary displays on the National Mall and Washington Monument, December 31, 2025 to January 5, 2026.

Did HJRES 133 pass? Where it stands

As of August 13, 2026, HJRES 133 has been signed into law.
