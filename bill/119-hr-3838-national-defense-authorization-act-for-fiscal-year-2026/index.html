Rep. Rogers, Mike D. (R-AL) · 1 cosponsor · 947 pages

What does the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2026 do?

HR 3838 is a House bill sponsored by Rep. Mike D. Rogers (R-AL). Sets fiscal year 2026 policy and authorizations for the Department of Defense, military construction, and the Department of Energy's national security programs, with funding tables authorizing about $881.85B in total (the sum of the seven table titles). It also folds in the SPEED Act acquisition overhaul, bars the Federal Reserve from issuing a central bank digital currency, and reauthorizes the Coast Guard through 2029.

Did HR 3838 pass? Where it stands

As of August 13, 2026, HR 3838 has passed the House.

Status: Passed House

Latest vote: House Passed 231–196 on September 10, 2025

Outlook: Likely

Key provisions

  • Division A — Defense Authorizations
    • Sets FY2026 DoD policy and end strengths (1,302,800 active-duty)
    • Bars DoD diversity, equity, and inclusion offices; merit-only personnel actions
    • Golden Dome missile-defense program and an ICBM floor of 400
  • Division B — Military Construction
    • Itemized construction projects sum to about $15.08B
    • Base-housing reforms: mold, radon, and independent inspections
    • Renames two Georgia Army bases; single-sex facility rule on installations
  • Division C — DOE Nuclear and Maritime
    • Plutonium pit floors: 30/yr at Los Alamos, 50/yr at Savannah River
    • New rapid program to develop modified nuclear weapons
    • $1.65B Maritime Administration; Chinese port-crane replacement
  • Division D — Funding Tables
    • $295.08B operation and maintenance; $181.05B military personnel
    • $153.23B procurement; $142.44B research and development
    • $33.64B Department of Energy atomic-energy defense activities
  • Division E — Federal Reserve CBDC Ban
    • Bars the Federal Reserve from issuing a central bank digital currency
    • Prohibits Fed retail accounts or products for individuals
  • Division F — Coast Guard Reauthorization
    • Reauthorizes Coast Guard appropriations through FY2029
    • Main appropriation line rises to $15.50B by FY2029
