Doug LaMalfa is the U.S. Representative for California's 1st District (CA-1).

District Geography

District covers all of Butte County, Colusa County, Glenn County, Lassen County, Modoc County, Shasta County, Siskiyou County, Sutter County, and Tehama County and part of Yuba County, including Chico, Redding, Yuba City, Oroville, and Susanville.

Biography

Douglas Lee LaMalfa was an American politician and businessman who served as the U.S. representative for California's 1st congressional district from 2013 until his death in 2026. A member of the Republican Party, his district covered nearly all of interior Northern California, including Chico, Redding, and Susanville.
