Stacey E. Plaskett is the U.S. Representative for U.S. Virgin Islands.

District Geography

District covers all of St. Croix Island, St. John Island, and St. Thomas Island, including Cruz Bay, Charlotte Amalie West, Coral Bay, Tutu, and Charlotte Amalie.

District boundaries: 119th Congress · U.S. Census Bureau relationship files.
Biography

Stacey Elizabeth Plaskett is an American politician and attorney serving since 2015 as the delegate to the United States House of Representatives from the United States Virgin Islands' at-large congressional district. Plaskett has practiced law in New York City, Washington, D.C., and the U.S. Virgin Islands.
