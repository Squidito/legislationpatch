Public Law 119-19
119th Congress

Joint Resolution

Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5,
United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Office of the
Comptroller of the Currency of the Department of the Treasury relating
to the review of applications under the Bank Merger Act. <<NOTE: June
20, 2025 - [S.J. Res. 13]>>

Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United
States of America in Congress assembled, That Congress disapproves the
rule submitted by the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency of the
Department of the Treasury relating to ``Business Combinations Under the
Bank Merger Act'' (89 Fed. Reg. 78207 (September 25, 2024)), and such
rule shall have no force or effect.

Approved June 20, 2025.
