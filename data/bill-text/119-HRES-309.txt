IN THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

April 9, 2025

Mr. Steil, from the Committee on House Administration, reported the
following resolution; which was referred to the House Calendar and
ordered to be printed

_______________________________________________________________________

RESOLUTION

Dismissing the election contest relating to the office of
Representative from the Twenty-eighth Congressional District of Texas.

Resolved, That the election contest relating to the office of
Representative from the Twenty-eighth Congressional District of Texas
is dismissed on the grounds of untimely filing with the House of
Representatives.
