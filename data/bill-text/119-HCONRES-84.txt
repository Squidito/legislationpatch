CONCURRENT RESOLUTION

Directing the President pursuant to section 5(c) of the War Powers
Resolution to remove United States Armed Forces from Lebanon.

Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),

removal of united states armed forces from lebanon

Section 1.
Pursuant to section 5(c) of the War Powers Resolution (50 U.S.C.
1544(c)), Congress directs the President to remove the United States
Armed Forces from Lebanon by not later than the date that is 7 days
after the date of the adoption of this concurrent resolution.
