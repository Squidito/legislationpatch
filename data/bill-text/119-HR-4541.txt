A BILL

To reauthorize the Young Women's Breast Health Education and Awareness
Requires Learning Young Act of 2009.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

This Act may be cited as the ``EARLY Act Reauthorization of 2025''.

SEC. 2. REAUTHORIZATION OF THE YOUNG WOMEN'S BREAST HEALTH EDUCATION
AND AWARENESS REQUIRES LEARNING YOUNG ACT OF 2009.

Section 399NN(h) of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C.
280m(h)) is amended by striking ``2026'' and inserting ``2031''.
