RESOLUTION

Dismissing the election contest relating to the office of
Representative from the at-large Congressional District of Alaska.

Resolved, That the election contest relating to the office of
Representative from the at-large Congressional District of Alaska is
dismissed on the grounds that, under section 2(1) of the Federal
Contested Election Act (2 U.S.C. 381(1)), the House of Representatives
has jurisdiction over official general and special elections to choose
a Representative in, or Delegate or Resident Commissioner to, the
Congress, but does not have jurisdiction over a primary election, or a
caucus or convention of a political party.
