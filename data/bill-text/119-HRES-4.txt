H. Res. 4

In the House of Representatives, U. S.,

January 3, 2025.
Resolved, That the Clerk be instructed to inform the President of the United
States that the House of Representatives has elected Mike Johnson, a
Representative from the State of Louisiana as Speaker, and Kevin McCumber, a
citizen of the State of Illinois as Clerk, of the House of Representatives of
the One Hundred Nineteenth Congress.
Attest:

Clerk.
