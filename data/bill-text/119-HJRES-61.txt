Public Law 119-14
119th Congress

Joint Resolution

Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5,
United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Environmental
Protection Agency relating to ``National Emission Standards for
Hazardous Air Pollutants: Rubber Tire Manufacturing''. <<NOTE: May 23,
2025 - [H.J. Res. 61]>>

Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United
States of America in Congress assembled, That Congress disapproves the
rule submitted by the Environmental Protection Agency relating to
``National Emission Standards for Hazardous Air Pollutants: Rubber Tire
Manufacturing'' (89 Fed. Reg. 94886 (November 29, 2024)), and such rule
shall have no force or effect.

Approved May 23, 2025.
