United States Code, 2024 Edition Title 5 - GOVERNMENT ORGANIZATION AND EMPLOYEES PART I - THE AGENCIES GENERALLY CHAPTER 1 - ORGANIZATION Sec. 105 - Executive agency From the U.S. Government Publishing Office, www.gpo.gov &sect;105. Executive agency For the purpose of this title, &quot;Executive agency&quot; means an Executive department, a Government corporation, and an independent establishment. (Pub. L. 89&ndash;554, Sept. 6, 1966, 80 Stat. 379.) Historical and Revision Notes The section is supplied to avoid the necessity for defining &quot;Executive agency&quot; each time it is used in this title.
