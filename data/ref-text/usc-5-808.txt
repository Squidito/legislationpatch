United States Code, 2024 Edition Title 5 - GOVERNMENT ORGANIZATION AND EMPLOYEES PART I - THE AGENCIES GENERALLY CHAPTER 8 - CONGRESSIONAL REVIEW OF AGENCY RULEMAKING Sec. 808 - Effective date of certain rules From the U.S. Government Publishing Office, www.gpo.gov &sect;808. Effective date of certain rules Notwithstanding section 801&mdash; (1) any rule that establishes, modifies, opens, closes, or conducts a regulatory program for a commercial, recreational, or subsistence activity related to hunting, fishing, or camping, or (2) any rule which an agency for good cause finds (and incorporates the finding and a brief statement of reasons therefor in the rule issued) that notice and public procedure thereon are impracticable, unnecessary, or contrary to the public interest, shall take effect at such time as the Federal agency promulgating the rule determines. (Added Pub. L. 104&ndash;121, title II, &sect;251, Mar. 29, 1996, 110 Stat. 874.)
