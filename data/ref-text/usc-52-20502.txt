United States Code, 2024 Edition Title 52 - VOTING AND ELECTIONS Subtitle II - Voting Assistance and Election Administration CHAPTER 205 - NATIONAL VOTER REGISTRATION Sec. 20502 - Definitions From the U.S. Government Publishing Office, www.gpo.gov &sect;20502. Definitions As used in this chapter&mdash; (1) the term &quot;election&quot; has the meaning stated in section 30101(1) of this title; (2) the term &quot;Federal office&quot; has the meaning stated in section 30101(3) of this title; (3) the term &quot;motor vehicle driver's license&quot; includes any personal identification document issued by a State motor vehicle authority; (4) the term &quot;State&quot; means a State of the United States and the District of Columbia; and (5) the term &quot;voter registration agency&quot; means an office designated under section 20506(a)(1) of this title to perform voter registration activities. (Pub. L. 103&ndash;31, &sect;3, May 20, 1993, 107 Stat. 77.) Editorial Notes Codification Section was formerly classified to section 1973gg&ndash;1 of Title 42, The Public Health and Welfare, prior to editorial reclassification and renumbering as this section.
