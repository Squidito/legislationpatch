United States Code, 2024 Edition Title 16 - CONSERVATION CHAPTER 90 - SECURE RURAL SCHOOLS AND COMMUNITY SELF-DETERMINATION SUBCHAPTER III - COUNTY FUNDS Sec. 7141 - Definitions From the U.S. Government Publishing Office, www.gpo.gov &sect;7141. Definitions In this subchapter: (1) County funds The term &quot;county funds&quot; means all funds an eligible county elects under section 7112(d) of this title to reserve for expenditure in accordance with this subchapter. (2) Participating county The term &quot;participating county&quot; means an eligible county that elects under section 7112(d) of this title to expend a portion of the Federal funds received under section 7112 of this title in accordance with this subchapter. (Pub. L. 106&ndash;393, title III, &sect;301, as added Pub. L. 110&ndash;343, div. C, title VI, &sect;601(a), Oct. 3, 2008, 122 Stat. 3909.)
