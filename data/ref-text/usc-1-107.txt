United States Code, 2024 Edition Title 1 - GENERAL PROVISIONS CHAPTER 2 - ACTS AND RESOLUTIONS; FORMALITIES OF ENACTMENT; REPEALS; SEALING OF INSTRUMENTS Sec. 107 - Parchment or paper for printing enrolled bills or resolutions From the U.S. Government Publishing Office, www.gpo.gov &sect;107. Parchment or paper for printing enrolled bills or resolutions Enrolled bills and resolutions of either House of Congress shall be printed on parchment or paper of suitable quality as shall be determined by the Joint Committee on Printing. (July 30, 1947, ch. 388, 61 Stat. 635.)
