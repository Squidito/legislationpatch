The Compilation of Presidential Documents (CPD) collection is the official publication of materials released by the White House Press Secretary. It comprises the Daily Compilation of Presidential Documents (January 20, 2009 – present) and its predecessor, the Weekly Compilation of Presidential Documents (with electronic access from 1993 –January 20, 2009). The Compilation of Presidential Documents is published by the Office of the Federal Register (OFR), National Archives and Records Administration (NARA) under the authority of the Federal Register Act (44 U.S.C. Ch. 15; 1 CFR part 10). Updated regularly by OFR editors as information is released by the White House Press Office, the Compilation of Presidential Documents includes such material as: Speeches and remarks News conferences Communications to Congress and Federal agencies Statements regarding bill signings and vetoes Proclamations Executive orders Appointments and nominations Resignations and retirements It also includes the following supplementary material compiled by OFR editors: Acts approved by the President Checklist of White House Press Releases Digest of Other White House Announcements Nominations Submitted to the Senate How are documents prepared for publication? Documents published in the Compilation of Presidential Documents are reviewed for accuracy and annotated with additional information by OFR editors to provide an authoritative record of the Presidency. Every effort is made to ensure accuracy: Remarks are checked against an audio or video recording (unless audio/video is unavailable, in which case this will be noted), and signed documents are checked against the original (when available, otherwise noted). Notes are provided by the editors for purposes of identification and clarity. The materials are organized in chronological order, and the dates shown in the headings are the dates of the documents or events. When the White House Press Office release date differs from the date of the document or event itself, this is indicated in the note at the end of the document. Remarks were delivered in Washington, DC, unless otherwise indicated. The times noted are local times. Category, subject, and name indexes are included in each document to enhance online search efforts. How do I cite the CPD? Suggested citation form: Daily Comp. Pres. Docs., 2025 DCPD No. 000103, p. 1. The citation is to material published in the Daily Compilation of Presidential Documents in the year 2025, in the first document, in the 103rd document, on page 1. Publication Background The Weekly Compilation of Presidential Documents was introduced in 1965 to provide a broad range of Presidential materials to the public in a timely manner. On January 20, 2009, the print-format Weekly Compilation of Presidential Documents was superseded by the online Daily Compilation of Presidential Documents to provide more immediate access to Presidential documents by making materials available in electronic format on a daily basis.
