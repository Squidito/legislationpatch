United States Code, 2024 Edition Title 2 - THE CONGRESS CHAPTER 19 - CONGRESSIONAL AWARD PROGRAM SUBCHAPTER I - CONGRESSIONAL AWARD PROGRAM Sec. 801 - Establishment, etc., of Congressional Award Board From the U.S. Government Publishing Office, www.gpo.gov &sect;801. Establishment, etc., of Congressional Award Board There is established a board to be known as the Congressional Award Board (hereinafter in this subchapter referred to as the &quot;Board&quot;), which shall be responsible for administering the Congressional Award Program described under section 802 of this title. The Board shall not be an agency or instrumentality of the United States, and the United States is not liable for any obligation or liability incurred by the Board. (Pub.
