United States Code, 2024 Edition Title 33 - NAVIGATION AND NAVIGABLE WATERS CHAPTER 22 - SEA GRANT COLLEGES AND MARINE SCIENCE DEVELOPMENT SUBCHAPTER III - YOUNG FISHERMEN'S DEVELOPMENT Sec. 1141 - Definitions From the U.S. Government Publishing Office, www.gpo.gov &sect;1141. Definitions In this subchapter: (1) Sea Grant Institution The term &quot;Sea Grant Institution&quot; means a sea grant college or sea grant institute, as those terms are defined in section 1122 of this title. (2) Tribal organization The term &quot;Tribal organization&quot; has the meaning given the term &quot;tribal organization&quot; in section 5304 of title 25. (3) Young fisherman The term &quot;young fisherman&quot; means an individual who&mdash; (A) desires to participate in the commercial fisheries of the United States, including the Great Lakes fisheries; (B) has worked as a captain, crew member, or deckhand on a commercial fishing vessel for not more than 10 years of cumulative service; or (C) is a beginning commercial fisherman. (Pub. L. 116&ndash;289, &sect;2, Jan. 5, 2021, 134 Stat. 4886.) Editorial Notes Codification Section was enacted as part of the Young Fishermen's Development Act, and not as part of titles I and II of Pub. L. 89&ndash;454 which comprise this chapter.
