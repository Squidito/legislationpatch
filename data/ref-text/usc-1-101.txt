United States Code, 2024 Edition Title 1 - GENERAL PROVISIONS CHAPTER 2 - ACTS AND RESOLUTIONS; FORMALITIES OF ENACTMENT; REPEALS; SEALING OF INSTRUMENTS Sec. 101 - Enacting clause From the U.S. Government Publishing Office, www.gpo.gov &sect;101. Enacting clause The enacting clause of all Acts of Congress shall be in the following form: &quot;Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled.&quot; (July 30, 1947, ch. 388, 61 Stat. 634.)
