United States Code, 2024 Edition Title 5 - GOVERNMENT ORGANIZATION AND EMPLOYEES PART I - THE AGENCIES GENERALLY CHAPTER 8 - CONGRESSIONAL REVIEW OF AGENCY RULEMAKING Sec. 807 - Exemption for monetary policy From the U.S. Government Publishing Office, www.gpo.gov &sect;807. Exemption for monetary policy Nothing in this chapter shall apply to rules that concern monetary policy proposed or implemented by the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System or the Federal Open Market Committee. (Added Pub. L. 104&ndash;121, title II, &sect;251, Mar. 29, 1996, 110 Stat. 874.)
