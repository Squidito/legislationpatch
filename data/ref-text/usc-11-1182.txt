United States Code, 2024 Edition Title 11 - BANKRUPTCY CHAPTER 11 - REORGANIZATION SUBCHAPTER V - SMALL BUSINESS DEBTOR REORGANIZATION Sec. 1182 - Definitions From the U.S. Government Publishing Office, www.gpo.gov &sect;1182. Definitions In this subchapter: (1) Debtor .&mdash;The term &quot;debtor&quot; means a small business debtor. (2) Debtor in possession .&mdash;The term &quot;debtor in possession&quot; means the debtor, unless removed as debtor in possession under section 1185(a) of this title. (Added Pub.
