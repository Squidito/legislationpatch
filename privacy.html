Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Information We Collect

LegislationPatch is designed to be as private as possible. We do not require you to create an account and we do not store your personal information on our servers.

IP Geolocation (Representative Tracker)

To automatically show your local congressional representatives, we use a third-party service (ipapi.co) to approximate your state based on your IP address. This data is processed in real-time and is not stored by LegislationPatch.

Local Storage

Any settings you configure — such as your tracked representatives, saved bills, or dark mode preference — are stored entirely locally in your browser using localStorage. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us.

Third-Party APIs

We use the Congress.gov API for all bill and legislative data. No personally identifying information is sent to this service.

Contact

If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact us.

Data directly sourced only from the Congress.gov API

LegislationPatch is nonpartisan and does not endorse any bill or political position.
